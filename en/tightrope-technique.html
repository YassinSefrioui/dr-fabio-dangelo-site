What Is the Endoscopic TightRope® Technique?

Every foot is unique, and no single surgical technique is appropriate for every patient. Dr. Fabio D'Angelo offers a comprehensive range of advanced surgical treatments, selecting the most suitable approach based on each patient's anatomy, lifestyle, and goals. His philosophy is to restore function, relieve pain, and preserve the natural biomechanics of the foot whenever possible.

Endoscopic TightRope® bunion surgery

An innovative minimally invasive procedure designed to correct selected bunions while minimizing soft tissue disruption. Performed through an endoscopic approach, this technique aims to restore alignment with smaller incisions and facilitate recovery in appropriately selected patients.

Bunion surgery isn't just about correcting a deformity. It's about restoring comfort, confidence, and freedom of movement.

Whether your goal is walking pain-free on holiday, enjoying an active lifestyle, or yes, occasionally slipping into your favourite pair of heels again, treatment is tailored to help you return to the activities you love.

What is the TightRope® Technique?

The TightRope® technique is an innovative method of correcting selected bunions using the Mini TightRope® System, developed by Arthrex, a global leader in orthopedic surgical technology.

Unlike many traditional procedures that rely on larger bone cuts and rigid fixation with screws, the TightRope® system uses a high-strength suture construct to restore alignment while preserving the natural relationship between the first and second metatarsal bones.

For appropriately selected patients, this can provide a stable correction through a minimally invasive approach while maintaining the natural biomechanics of the foot.

What Makes Dr. D'Angelo's Technique Different?

This is where Dr. D'Angelo's expertise sets him apart.

While a small number of surgeons perform the TightRope® procedure, Dr. Fabio D'Angelo is the only surgeon performing it using a fully endoscopic technique.

By combining endoscopic surgery with the Mini TightRope® system, he is able to perform the procedure through very small incisions while directly visualizing the anatomy throughout the operation.

Dr. D'Angelo has also been invited to teach this advanced technique to surgeons across Europe, helping expand the adoption of endoscopic foot surgery through specialized educational courses and hands-on training.

Why Choose Endoscopic TightRope®?

  • Minimally invasive
  • Smaller incisions
  • Less disruption of soft tissues
  • Precise correction
  • Preservation of normal foot anatomy
  • Earlier functional recovery for appropriate patients
  • Minimal scarring
  • Designed to restore comfort and natural movement

Is Endoscopic TightRope® Right for Me?

Following a detailed clinical examination and X-rays, Dr. D'Angelo will determine whether the endoscopic TightRope® technique is the most appropriate treatment for your foot. Factors considered include:

  • Severity of the bunion
  • Joint mobility
  • Bone quality
  • Activity level
  • Age
  • Lifestyle
  • Previous surgery

Recovery

Typical recovery milestones:

Week 1

Protected weight-bearing according to your individualized recovery plan.

Weeks 2–4

Swelling gradually decreases, and walking becomes progressively more comfortable.

Around 6 Weeks

Many patients resume most normal daily activities, depending on healing and the type of work they do.

3–6 Months

The foot continues to recover, with swelling steadily improving and function returning.

Recovery varies from person to person, and Dr. D'Angelo provides a personalized rehabilitation plan for every patient.
