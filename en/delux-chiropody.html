Delux Chiropody

Delux Chiropody is a new formula from FD-Foot Clinic: the most complete and specific treatment for any foot problem, performed by the best specialists.

It combines aesthetic chiropody with podiatric physiotherapy to achieve the complete wellbeing of the foot, uniting the clinical excellence of our podiatrists and physiotherapists with the finest cosmetic products.

Thanks to organic products, this treatment relieves stress, relaxes the muscles of the foot and helps release accumulated tension.

With over 15 years of experience, we provide advanced, comprehensive treatments dedicated solely to the feet, for men and women of all ages.
