Aesthetic Chiropody

Performed by our qualified podiatrists, aesthetic chiropody is the best way to improve the appearance of the feet and nails.

After a meticulous examination, our podiatrists can detect foot pathologies responsible for pain. These patients are then seen together with Dr. Fabio D'Angelo to provide the best medical-aesthetic or surgical response.

In the absence of associated pathologies, it is carried out with specifically designed instruments, in complete hygiene and safety, with the foot dry, to guarantee excellent and lasting results.

We use only our FD-cosmetics formulations, with anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, antifungal, anti-ageing and moisturising properties for daily care.

  • 30 minutes with our qualified podiatrist
  • Only FD-cosmetics products
  • Specific, sterilised instruments
