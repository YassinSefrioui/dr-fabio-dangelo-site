Introducing the SPARE™ Method

The SPARE™ Method

Surgery Preserving Anatomy, Restoring, Endoscopically.

Pioneered and refined by Dr. Fabio D'Angelo, the SPARE™ Method is an innovative surgical method built on one fundamental principle: preserving healthy anatomy whenever clinically appropriate while restoring natural function through advanced endoscopic and minimally invasive techniques.

The SPARE™ Method is especially relevant in the treatment of bunions (hallux valgus), where many traditional corrective techniques require an osteotomy — a surgical procedure in which the bone is cut and realigned to correct the deformity. Through his pioneering endoscopic expertise, Dr. D'Angelo is able to correct a broad spectrum of bunion deformities while preserving healthy anatomy whenever clinically appropriate.

The SPARE™ Method incorporates advanced techniques, including Endoscopic TightRope® bunion correction, as part of a personalized surgical plan designed to achieve the best possible long-term functional outcome.

Beyond bunion correction, the SPARE™ Method guides Dr. D'Angelo's approach to revision surgery, forefoot reconstruction, tendon disorders, sports injuries, and other complex foot and ankle conditions.
