The future of bunion surgery is here

Endoscopic TightRope® bunion surgery is the best-in-class minimally invasive bunion correction.

A bunion (hallux valgus) is one of the most common foot deformities, affecting millions of people worldwide. As the condition progresses, it can cause pain, difficulty finding comfortable footwear, reduced mobility, and a significant impact on quality of life.

Although the TightRope® procedure has gained increasing recognition, it is performed by only a small number of highly specialized foot and ankle surgeons worldwide. Dr. Fabio D'Angelo is currently the only surgeon performing the TightRope® bunion procedure using a fully endoscopic technique, combining the precision of endoscopic surgery with the benefits of the TightRope® system.

Recognized as a leader in this field, Dr. D'Angelo has also been invited to teach surgeons across Europe, sharing his expertise through specialized training courses on the endoscopic TightRope® technique.

Benefits of endoscopic TightRope® bunion surgery

  • Smaller incisions and minimal scarring
  • Reduced soft tissue disruption
  • Less postoperative pain
  • Earlier functional recovery for appropriately selected patients
  • Restoration of the natural alignment and biomechanics of the foot

If bunion pain is affecting your daily life, a specialist consultation can help determine whether endoscopic TightRope® surgery is the right treatment for you.
