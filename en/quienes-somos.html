01 · About Dr. D'Angelo

My Mission

At Dr. Fabio D'Angelo's practice, the mission is to redefine foot and ankle surgery through innovation, precision, and compassionate patient care.

We are committed to advancing the field through pioneering endoscopic and minimally invasive techniques while preserving healthy anatomy whenever clinically appropriate and delivering highly personalized treatment for every patient.

Central to our philosophy is the SPARE™ Method — Surgery Preserving Anatomy, Restoring, Endoscopically — a surgical method pioneered and refined by Dr. D'Angelo. More than a surgical technique, it represents a commitment to meticulous planning, anatomical preservation, surgical precision, and selecting the most appropriate approach for each individual patient.

As an internationally recognized foot and ankle surgeon, Dr. D'Angelo welcomes patients from around the world. Fluent in Italian, Spanish, French, and English, he is able to communicate directly with patients in their preferred language. Members of our team are also pleased to assist patients in Arabic, ensuring that every individual feels welcomed, understood, and supported throughout every stage of their care.

Whether treating local patients or those traveling internationally for specialized foot surgery, our mission remains the same: to combine surgical excellence with innovation, precision, and compassionate care to help every patient move forward with confidence.

02 · International career

Professional Journey

Over the past two decades, Dr. Fabio D'Angelo has built an international career across Italy, Spain, France, Germany, and the United States, holding leadership positions at some of Europe's most respected hospitals while contributing to surgical education and innovation worldwide.

Chief of Orthopaedic Surgery

Clínica Planas · Barcelona, Spain · 2018–Present

As Chief of Orthopaedic Surgery, Dr. D'Angelo leads one of Spain's premier private surgical departments, specializing exclusively in minimally invasive, endoscopic, and aesthetic foot surgery. He has pioneered advanced procedures that reduce surgical trauma, accelerate recovery, and improve functional and cosmetic outcomes for patients from around the world.

International Consultant & Instructor

Arthrex GmbH · Germany · 2018–Present

Recognized internationally for his expertise, Dr. D'Angelo serves as a consultant and surgical instructor for Arthrex, one of the world's leading orthopaedic medical technology companies. He trains orthopaedic surgeons in advanced minimally invasive and endoscopic foot surgery techniques through international courses, cadaveric laboratories, and live surgical demonstrations.

Adjunct Professor

Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) · Spain · 2018–2019

Faculty member for postgraduate education in reconstructive and aesthetic surgery, mentoring surgeons and sharing advanced surgical techniques with the next generation of specialists.

Orthopaedic & Trauma Surgeon

ELSAN Group · France · 2014–2017

Practiced within France's largest private hospital network, treating complex orthopaedic and trauma conditions while expanding expertise in advanced reconstructive surgery and minimally invasive techniques.

Head of Orthopaedic Department

Quirónsalud · Spain · 2010–2014

Led the Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery Department at one of Spain's leading private healthcare groups, managing complex surgical cases while introducing innovative patient-centered treatment approaches.

Orthopaedic & Trauma Surgeon

Clínica Sanatrix · Rome, Italy · 2009–2010

Started his consultant career in orthopaedic and trauma surgery, developing extensive experience in reconstructive procedures and comprehensive patient care before moving into international leadership positions.

03 · Academic background

Education & International Training

Dr. Fabio D'Angelo has completed an extensive international academic and surgical training pathway across Italy, Spain, and the United States, combining clinical excellence, academic research, and healthcare leadership.

His academic qualifications include:

  • Doctorate (PhD) in Sports Traumatology, UCAM — Catholic University of Murcia (Spain), awarded with Highest Honors.
  • Master's Degree in Healthcare Management, Centro de Estudios Financieros (CEF), Spain.
  • Specialist Certification in Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery, Sapienza University of Rome (Italy), awarded with Highest Honors.
  • Orthopaedic Surgery Fellowship, Harvard University (USA), where he further refined advanced orthopaedic surgical techniques.
  • Doctor of Medicine (MD), Sapienza University of Rome (Italy), graduated with Highest Honors.
