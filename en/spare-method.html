The SPARE™ Method

Surgery Preserving Anatomy, Restoring, Endoscopically.

Pioneered and refined by Dr. Fabio D'Angelo, the SPARE™ Method is an innovative surgical approach that rethinks how foot and ankle surgery should be performed. Rather than focusing solely on correcting a deformity, the SPARE™ Method is built on the principle of preserving healthy anatomy whenever clinically appropriate while restoring natural function through advanced endoscopic and minimally invasive techniques.

Every patient is unique. Every foot is unique. For this reason, the SPARE™ Method is not defined by a single procedure, but by a consistent commitment to preserving anatomy, restoring function, and selecting the most appropriate surgical technique for each individual patient.

Rather than adapting the patient to a procedure, the SPARE™ Method adapts the procedure to the patient.

Why the SPARE™ Method?

While the SPARE™ Method guides Dr. Fabio D'Angelo's approach to a wide range of foot and ankle conditions, it is particularly transformative in the treatment of bunions (hallux valgus). This is because traditional bunion correction has historically relied on an osteotomy — a surgical procedure in which the bone is cut and repositioned to correct the deformity.

For many patients, the prospect of cutting the bone is one of the main reasons bunion surgery is delayed or avoided. Because the bone must then heal in its new position, recovery involves a prolonged healing period, swelling, protected weight-bearing, and gradual rehabilitation before patients can return to normal activities.

The SPARE™ Method was developed to challenge the traditional approach. By preserving healthy anatomy and avoiding unnecessary bone cutting whenever clinically appropriate, it combines advanced endoscopic and minimally invasive techniques to reduce surgical trauma while restoring alignment, stability, and natural foot function. For appropriately selected patients, this innovative approach allows Dr. D'Angelo to treat a broad spectrum of bunion deformities while preserving healthy anatomy whenever clinically appropriate.

The goal is not simply to correct the deformity, but to help patients recover with greater comfort and confidence while achieving durable, long-term functional outcomes.

The Five Principles of SPARE™

S — Surgery

Every procedure begins with meticulous planning and an individualized assessment of the patient's anatomy, lifestyle, and goals.

P — Preserving

Preserving healthy bone, joints, ligaments, and soft tissues.

A — Anatomy

Respecting the natural anatomy and biomechanics of the foot rather than unnecessarily altering them.

R — Restoring

The objective is not simply correcting an X-ray, but restoring comfortable movement, stability, and quality of life.

E — Endoscopically

Using advanced endoscopic techniques to minimize surgical incisions.
